`Set Invisible(player, invisibleTo)`

Causes one or more players to become invisible to either all other players or just enemies.

| Argument | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `player` | `Player \| Array<Player>` | The player or players who will become invisible. |
| `invisibleTo` | `Invis` | Specifies for whom the player or players will be invisible. |

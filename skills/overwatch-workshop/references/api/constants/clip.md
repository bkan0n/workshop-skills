The built-in `Clip` enum.

| --- | --- | --- |
| `Do Not Clip` | `Clip.NONE` | The text will always be fully visible, even if it is behind a wall or solid object. |
| `Clip Against Surfaces` | `Clip.SURFACES` | The text may be partially or completely obscured by walls, floors, ceilings, players, or other solid objects. |
